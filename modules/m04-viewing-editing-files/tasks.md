# Viewing & Editing Files Tasks

**Objective:** Inspect and search the contents of a file — including one too large to read top to bottom
and one actively being written to — without opening it in a GUI.

> There is no solutions file for this stage. This checklist guides the work — it doesn't contain it. See
> `brief.md` for the full objective, scope, stack constraints, and definition of done, and fill in
> `write-up-template.md` as you go, not after.

## Setup

- [ ] Confirm with your trainer: what log source to use, if nothing suitable is available on your
      system.
- [ ] Choose your source log file and the pattern you'll filter on.
- [ ] Choose a terminal-based editor.

## Build

- [ ] Research the "Research:" questions under each Scope bullet in `brief.md` before you start.
- [ ] View your log whole, a page at a time, and from the top or bottom, and note which tool fit best.
- [ ] Search the log for your pattern and produce the filtered excerpt without trimming it by hand.
- [ ] Follow a file as it grows and capture evidence that you saw new lines arrive.
- [ ] Make at least one edit to a file directly in the terminal and capture the diff.

## Verify

- [ ] Confirm the excerpt contains only lines that match your stated pattern, with no manual trimming.
- [ ] Confirm you demonstrated following a growing file, not just a static one.
- [ ] Confirm you can show the diff from your terminal edit.

## Write-up

- [ ] Under "What I built," describe the log excerpt, the pattern, and the edit.
- [ ] Under "Why it's built this way," explain your source log, pattern, and editor choice.
- [ ] Under "How to build it," write a guide to viewing, searching, and editing from the terminal,
      including following a growing file.
- [ ] Under "Concepts worth explaining," pick 1–2 ideas (streaming vs. paginated viewing, or pattern
      search) and explain each in your own words.
- [ ] Under "What tripped me up," capture anything that didn't work as expected.
- [ ] Under "Checkpoint evidence," show the filtered excerpt, the command, and the diff.
- [ ] Under "What I'd do differently," reflect on what you'd change.
- [ ] Final self-review: re-read your work against M4's definition of done.
